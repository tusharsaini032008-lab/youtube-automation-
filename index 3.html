WHO WE ARE

About The Daily Desk

The Daily Desk is a fictional general-newsroom concept created as a college website project. It demonstrates how a digital news publication can organise stories across national, international, political, business, technology, sports and entertainment categories.

The sample content is for layout and learning purposes. A real newsroom should verify information, identify sources, provide context and correct errors transparently.
